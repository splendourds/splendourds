<div align="center">

**Splendour Digital Services** is a CAC-registered technology company established in **2018** *(RC 9450128)*, delivering software engineering, digital solutions, data analysis, and healthcare technology to institutions, businesses, and professionals across Nigeria and beyond.

Led by a **Full-Stack Software Developer and Registered Nurse**, we operate at the rare intersection of clinical insight and production-grade engineering — building systems that are technically robust, operationally practical, and clinically informed.

Our portfolio spans school management and examination systems, CBT platforms, business management systems, healthcare applications, electronic health records (EHR), SUG election software, and local-server applications — all engineered to perform in real-world environments.
