# Splendour Digital Services

---

## Profile Overview

**Splendour Digital Services** (CAC Registration Number: **RC 9450128**, established in 2018) is a technology-driven firm specializing in software engineering, digital solutions, data analysis, and healthcare technology. Based in Hadejia, Jigawa State, Nigeria, we design and deploy practical, real-world systems that address the technological needs of educational institutions, corporate businesses, healthcare professionals, and organizations.

Led by a full-stack software developer and Registered Nurse, our work bridges the gap between advanced technology and clinical, educational, and operational efficiency.

---

## Core Specializations

- **Software Engineering & Web Architecture:** Full-stack development, offline-first software, and local-server network systems.
- **Healthcare Technology:** Specialized software supporting nursing activities, clinical reasoning, patient care planning, and clinical data presentation.
- **Educational Technology:** Multi-level school management platforms, examination management systems, computer-based testing (CBT) software, and electronic voting applications.
- **Business Solutions:** Custom business management systems and POS applications for retail and enterprise operations.
- **Data Solutions (SplendourStat):** Statistical analysis, data visualization, and professional data interpretation tools.

---

## Featured Software Solutions & Projects

- **ECast App:** Student Union Government (SUG) electronic voting software developed for the Jigawa State College of Nursing Sciences, Birnin Kudu Campus.
- **School Exam Manager:** Comprehensive examination management software implemented at Model Boarding Primary and Junior Secondary School, Guri.
- **SplendourCare Clinical Suite:** A nursing-focused clinical support solution designed to assist with clinical reasoning, diagnosis, patient care planning, quality-of-care monitoring, and clinical trend visualization.
- **Business Management Systems:** Custom operational software implemented for corporate and retail clients including Liberty Computers, Hamza Me Mai Investment, MMY Collections, and Hadejia Computers.
- **SplendourStat:** A data analysis and presentation application focused on statistical computation, data interpretation, and professional charting.
- **Examination Management Systems v2.0:** Scalable examination solutions tailored to secondary schools and higher institutions.
- **Local-Server Applications:** Robust business and communication systems engineered for isolated local network environments.
